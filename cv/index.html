Academic profile

Curriculum vitae

Kenan Çolak is an urban and regional planner and urban climate researcher. He holds a PhD from Akdeniz University and also holds a law degree. He has more than eleven years of institutional experience in Türkiye in cultural heritage conservation, conservation-oriented zoning planning and the management of urban development. His research examines how urban morphology and its change relate to land surface temperature, using Landsat and Sentinel imagery, land cover classification, spatial statistics and temporal change analysis. His doctoral research in Antalya develops a controlled comparison approach built on areas whose physical characteristics remain stable, and a current project examines redevelopment around SkyTrain stations in Metro Vancouver. He is interested in translating urban climate evidence into concrete planning decisions and is open to postdoctoral collaboration.
