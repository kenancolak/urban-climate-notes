Research profile

Urban planner with a PhD in Urban and Regional Planning (2026) and more than 11 years of experience in public-sector planning and heritage conservation. I use satellite observations, GIS and statistical methods to study urban morphology, land-cover change and surface temperature. My doctoral research examined thermal change across stable urban and forest surfaces in Antalya. My current research investigates how redevelopment changes urban form, vegetation and surface temperature.

Pursued academic research alongside public-sector planning responsibilities, working with planners, architects, engineers and other specialists on heritage conservation and urban development.

Selected research

Independent Researcher | Canada | 2023-present

Continuing research on urban morphology and the surface urban heat island, focusing on how redevelopment, new development, and physical decline alter building form, vegetation, and land cover. Developing longitudinal comparisons with morphologically similar stable areas to examine the thermal consequences of these changes and their implications for planning.

Pedestrian movement in historic urban environments | Master’s thesis

Examined pedestrian movement in Antalya-Kaleiçi using space syntax, pedestrian counts and density analysis.
